Cooking Tips

🍌 Ripeness Matters

Use ripe bananas with brown spots for baking - they're sweeter and easier to mash!

❄️ Freeze for Later

Peel and freeze overripe bananas for smoothies and baking. They'll last for months!

🥄 Natural Sweetener

Mashed bananas can replace sugar in many recipes, making them healthier!

🥚 Egg Substitute

One mashed banana can replace one egg in vegan baking recipes!
